Watch a Product Price and Get Alerted at Your Target

A scheduled job checks a product page every few hours and messages you the moment the price drops below your target.

#price#monitor#alert#shopping#cron

What you need

  • Hermes Agentrequired
  • Telegram Botoptional

Pick the product and your target price

Find the product page URL. Note the current price and the price you would actually buy at. Be realistic — a target that never triggers is useless.

Ask your agent to set up the watch

Describe the watch in one sentence. The agent creates a cron job that fetches the page, extracts the price, and compares it against your target.

text
Check this product page every 3 hours:
https://example.com/product/12345
If the price is at or below 89,000 won,
message me with the price and the link, then stop watching.

Make the job silent when nothing happens

The job must not message you every check. Only alert on two events: the price hit the target, or the page broke (product removed or price selector changed) so you know the watch is dead.

Test it once

Set the target temporarily above the current price to confirm the alert path works end to end, then set it back to your real target.
