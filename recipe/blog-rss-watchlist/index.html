Track Your Favorite Blogs Without Opening Them

Subscribe to a list of blogs and RSS feeds, and receive one digest a day with only the new posts.

#rss#blog#digest#feeds#cron

What you need

  • Hermes Agentrequired

Collect your feed list

Write down 5–15 blogs you actually read. For each one find the RSS or Atom feed URL — usually at /feed, /rss.xml, or linked from the footer.

text
https://example-blog.com/feed
https://another.dev/rss.xml
https://newsletter.site.io/atom.xml

Create the watchlist job

Ask the agent to store the feed list and check it once a day. It keeps state of what it already reported, so you only see new posts.

text
Every day at 8 AM, check these feeds and send me a digest
of posts published since the last check. One line per post:
title — blog name. Skip posts older than 7 days.

Tune the noise level

After a week, prune feeds that never interest you and add a rule like "group by topic" or "max 10 items" if the digest gets long. A watchlist you stop reading is dead weight.
